Three Kinds of Weight

The Trusted sources panel has one slider doing three unrelated jobs — and for five of nine rows, doing nothing at all.

“We have a few types of weights — one for news wires and another for top-300 draft picks toward draft analyzer. Sleepers should have a weight based on info given (website post or a list given by users). Let's take a step back and architect this the right way.” — owner, Aug 21

The problem

One abstraction, three jobs

frontend/index.html:1409 defines a single SOURCES array — ten entries, one weight each, one slider each, one percentage each. The percentage is a share of whatever is switched on:

influence(x) = weightOf(x) / Σ weightOf(active) × 100 // :2178, :2187

But the entries are not the same kind of thing, and almost none of the weights reach an output.

SourceKindWhat its weight does
Aggregate ADP · ESPN kit · Yahoo top-300rank listSums into trusted
My own tiersrank listSums into own
Team beat writersusage signalNothing — on/off only
Route and snap analyticsusage signalNothing — on/off only
NBC Sports · @AdamSchefter · Rotowirenews wireNothing at all

The four that count collapse into one scalar, which drives the whole board:

srcWeight = 100 × trusted / (trusted + own) // :1904 blendScore = (1 − w) × player.rank + w × player.adp // :2250

So five of nine active sliders move a bar and change no output. The panel says “weight sets how hard it pulls.” For most rows it does not pull. Under this repo's no false positives rule that is a defect, not a rough edge.

The deeper fault is the category error: a news wire and a top-300 ranking list cannot share a slider, because they share no unit, no consumer, and no truth condition. Schefter reporting a torn ACL and ESPN ranking a player 73rd are not two amounts of the same substance.

Foundations

What already exists, and is good

The server already has a real news scorer. This design does not replace it — it plugs weights into it.

ModuleWhat it already does
impact.pyBuckets each item severe / status / positive / noise; scores category (50/25/15/−40) plus who it is about; decays at 3.0/day; clusters the same story across outlets. Source identity is not part of the score.
sources.pySeven polled feeds, each with tier (fact vs analysis) and budget_hours. No trust value.
adp.pyLive ADP blended across 10- and 12-team; sleeper finds from a position-adjusted ADP-vs-rank gap; plus wire items that say “sleeper” and name a player.
scorecard.pyAn immutable ledger: records a call when it is made, grades it later against real box scores, reports calibration.

Two of these matter enormously. cluster() already knows when three outlets said the same thing — today it treats that purely as duplication to fold away. And scorecard.py already knows how to grade a claim. Neither is used by any weighting.

The design

Three families, three units

Three registries in one canonical module — app/feeds/weights.py — for the same reason app/leagues.py is the only place league facts live: a number that exists in two places will eventually disagree with itself. sources.py stays where to fetch; weights.py becomes how much to believe.

Wire trust — news

final = impact.score(item) × wire_trust(source) × corroboration(n) − decay(age)

Two changes to impact.py. order() factors in the outlet, so a Schefter status post outranks an identical-category post from a weaker source. And cluster() returns how many outlets carried the story — three independent outlets reporting the same injury is a stronger claim than one, so corroboration should raise the score sub-linearly (1 + k·log n), with the highest-trust outlet's copy shown.

Trust is separable from tier: tier says fact-vs-opinion, trust says how often this outlet is right and first.

Board trust — rankings into the analyzer

blended_rank(p) = Σ (wᵢ × rankᵢ(p)) / Σ wᵢ over lists that rank p

Only genuine rank lists belong here: Aggregate ADP, ESPN draft kit, Yahoo top-300, Sleeper search rank, and My own tiers. The wires do not — that is the category error, fixed. Today's srcWeight is just the two-list case of this.

The trap is missing players. A source that does not rank someone must not be read as ranking him 0, and must not silently drop him. Renormalize over the lists that do rank him — and surface how many did, because a player ranked by one list out of five is a different claim from one ranked by all five.

Sleeper evidence

The family the owner framed most precisely: weight based on the info given. A sleeper claim has provenance, and provenance sets confidence.

EvidenceSourceWhy it ranks there
Measured ADP gapadp.pyComputed from real draft rooms — strongest, because nobody's opinion is in it
Measured usagedepth.pyAlso measured; not yet joined to sleepers
Article mention_article_finds()Someone published it — trust flows from wire trust
User list★ button, /app/mineThe owner's own call: high trust, zero corroboration
confidence = evidence_base(kind) × source_trust × corroboration × recency

The point is to make evidence kinds comparable, so a sleeper backed by a measured 30-pick gap can outrank one backed by a listicle — and the card says which it is, instead of calling both “Sleeper find”.

The part that makes the weights honest

Every weight above is, today, an opinion typed into an array. The app already owns the machinery to turn them into measurements.

A sleeper call is the same shape as a TD lean: a claim, made at a known time, gradeable later against real production. Record sleeper and wire-driven calls into the ledger with their evidence kind and source attached, and after a few weeks of real games it can answer which sources and which evidence kinds were actually right.

The defaults then stop being declared and start being earned. That is the argument for architecting this rather than patching the sliders: it is the difference between a control panel and a measurement.

Sequence · each step depends on the one before

Phasing

Ordered so nothing ships claiming an influence it does not have.

  1. Stop lying

    Split the panel into three labelled groups; disable the sliders that feed nothing, each with a one-line statement of what it changes. Small, and it closes the false-positive defect immediately.

  2. Wire trust

    Trust values and corroboration in impact.py. Self-contained, and the Alerts board shows the effect straight away.

  3. Board aggregation

    Replace the scalar blend with weighted rank aggregation. The riskiest change — it reorders the draft board — so the mock-draft engine's headless test is its check.

  4. Sleeper evidence

    Evidence kinds and confidence on every find; each card states its provenance.

  5. Grade it

    Sleeper calls into the ledger; calibration by source and by evidence kind; defaults derived from the record.

Steps 1 and 2 are independently useful and do not depend on 3–5.

Decisions I should not make alone

Open questions

  1. Do wire weights belong to the user at all? Trust in Schefter is close to a fact about the world, not a preference. Either ship measured defaults and let users nudge, or keep wire trust owner-only and give users just board trust and sleeper evidence — the two that are genuinely about their league and their taste.
  2. How much should corroboration count? Three outlets on one story — worth 1.5× a single report, or 2×?
  3. Should your own tier list be able to win outright? Today srcWeight can go to zero (“My tiers only”). Keep that escape hatch in the N-list model?
