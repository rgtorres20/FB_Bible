Booking policies

Deposit

A $30 deposit holds your spot. Pay with Apple Pay, Cash App Pay, Google Pay, card, or Zelle. The balance is due at your appointment.

Late arrivals

There is a 10-minute grace period. After that, your appointment is canceled or rescheduled and a new deposit is required.

Refunds

Services are non-refundable. Final payment is due at the time of your appointment.

Cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours notice. Cancel or reschedule anytime from the link in your confirmation text.

Before your appointment

Come with a clean face, no makeup Skip caffeine before your visit No moisturizer around the eyes Remove contact lenses Please come alone, no extra guests Keep lashes dry for 24 hours after Dress comfortably, you'll be lying down
